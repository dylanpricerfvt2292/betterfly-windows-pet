## Meet BetterFly

BetterFly brings a virtual pet into your regular Windows workspace without taking over the desktop. The companion runs inside a transparent, borderless overlay and uses a dialogue engine to offer interactive commentary while staying lightweight in the background.

Its mood can change over time, and you decide how frequently it starts a conversation. Pets and skins provide visual customization, with more content available through the community store. Built-in automatic updates help keep the application current without a separate manual update process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does BetterFly do?

BetterFly is a Windows virtual pet and desktop companion. It appears in a desktop overlay and combines interactive dialogue with changing moods and customizable visual options.

### Is the conversation frequency adjustable?

Yes. The chat-frequency control lets you select a busier interaction pattern or a quieter one.

### Are pets and skins customizable?

Yes. You can change the companion's pets and skins, including content offered through the community store.

### Are updates automatic?

BetterFly supports over-the-air updates. Internet access may be needed to download update packages and community content.

### Can it open when Windows starts?

Yes. Launch on startup can be enabled as an optional application setting.

### How can I find the overlay if it is missing?

First verify that BetterFly is running. Then check whether the overlay was moved beyond the visible desktop and review the display or visibility options in the application. Restarting BetterFly may also resolve issues that appear after installation or an update.

### Where should I report problems?

Submit bug reports, configuration questions, and update issues through the repository's [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/dylanpricerfvt2292/betterfly-windows-pet/issues).
